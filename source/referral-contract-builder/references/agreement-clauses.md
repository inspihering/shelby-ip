# Referral Agreement — Clause Reference

Use these as drafting patterns. Adapt names, offers, and numbers to the specific deal; don't leave placeholder brackets in the final draft unless the user genuinely hasn't decided something yet (in which case flag it clearly, e.g., "[CONFIRM: payment window]").

## 1. Agreement Date & Parties
State the effective date and full legal/business names of both parties (e.g., "This Referral Agreement is entered into on [date] between [Business A / Owner Name] ('Referrer') and [Business B / Owner Name] ('Provider').").

## 2. Purpose of Agreement
One or two sentences: the parties wish to formalize a referral relationship in which one or both parties may refer prospective clients to the other in exchange for a referral fee.

## 3. Services Eligible for Referral
List the specific offer(s), each with: name, brief description, price point, and any exclusions (e.g., "Referral fees apply only to the Signature Keynote package, not to workshop bookings under $2,000").

## 4. Definition of a Qualified Referral
Be concrete: a referral is "qualified" when the prospect (a) was not already an existing client or in active conversation with the Provider, (b) was introduced by name with the Referrer's knowledge, and (c) [becomes a paying client / books a discovery call / signs a contract] within [X days] of introduction.

## 5. Referral Process
Simple numbered flow: partner identifies a prospect → confirms interest → makes a warm introduction (email/message/form) → receiving party confirms receipt → discovery call/sales process → referral marked qualified or not → if a client, sale is tracked → fee paid per terms.

## 6. Referral Fee or Commission
State the exact structure. Common patterns:
- Flat fee: "$[amount] per qualified referral that becomes a paying client."
- Percentage of first sale: "[X]% of the total value of the first invoice paid by the referred client."
- Percentage of recurring revenue: "[X]% of revenue collected from the referred client for [X months/years] from the date of first payment."
- Tiered: "[X]% on the first $[amount], [Y]% above that" or volume-based tiers.

## 7. Payment Terms
Specify: when the fee is *earned* (e.g., "upon the referred client's payment clearing"), when it's *paid* (e.g., "within 15 days of the end of the month in which payment clears"), and method (e.g., bank transfer, check, PayPal).

## 8. Refunds, Cancellations & Chargebacks
State whether a refund/chargeback claws back the referral fee already paid, and the process for that (e.g., "If the referred client receives a full refund within 30 days, the Provider may deduct the corresponding referral fee from the next payment cycle.").

## 9. Tracking & Reporting
Name the method both parties will use to log and confirm referrals (spreadsheet, CRM, shared form) and the cadence for reporting (e.g., monthly summary).

## 10. Confidentiality
Standard mutual confidentiality: both parties agree not to disclose client information, pricing, or business terms learned through the relationship, except as needed to complete the referral.

## 11. Brand Representation
Each party agrees to represent the other's business, offers, and pricing accurately and not to make promises on the other's behalf.

## 12. Client Relationship Ownership
Clarify who "owns" the client relationship going forward (typically the party delivering the service) and how much involvement the referring party retains, if any.

## 13. Non-Circumvention
Neither party will attempt to go around the other to deal directly with a referred client in a way that avoids the referral fee, for the term of the agreement plus [X months] after.

## 14. Independent Contractor Relationship
State clearly this is not an employment, partnership, or joint-venture relationship — each party is an independent contractor responsible for their own taxes, insurance, and liabilities.

## 15. Term & Termination
State the initial term (e.g., ongoing until terminated, or a fixed 12-month term with renewal) and how either party may terminate (e.g., "with 30 days' written notice"). Clarify that referrals made before termination are still eligible for payout per the agreed terms.

## 16. Dispute Resolution
A simple, non-litigious first step (e.g., "the parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute informally; unresolved disputes may be referred to mediation in [state/country]").

## 17. Entire Agreement
Standard clause: this document represents the entire agreement between the parties regarding referrals and supersedes prior verbal or written understandings on this topic.

## 18. Signature Lines
Name, signature, title (if applicable), and date for each party.

## Required disclaimer

Always include, verbatim:

> This is a business draft and not legal advice. Please have this agreement reviewed by a qualified attorney before using it with referral partners.
