# Referral Fee Guidance (Reference Ranges Only)

These are general starting anchors, not rules. Always remind the user every business model is different, and the right number depends on: profit margin, sales cycle length, delivery cost, relationship value, and how much work the referral partner does (a passive introduction vs. actively helping close the sale warrants different rates).

| Offer type | Typical referral fee range |
|---|---|
| Low-ticket digital product | 20%–50% |
| Workshop or event ticket | 10%–30% |
| Coaching or consulting package | 10%–25% |
| High-ticket program | 5%–20% |
| Membership | % of first payment, or a recurring % for a defined period |
| Corporate training / speaking contract | Flat finder's fee, or 5%–15% |

## Structure options to offer the user

- Flat referral fee
- Percentage of first sale only
- Percentage of recurring revenue for a defined period (e.g., 10% for 12 months)
- Tiered commission (rate increases with volume or deal size)
- Mutual referral exchange (no cash changes hands — reciprocal referrals instead)
- Affiliate-style payout (post-sale, automated)
- One-time finder's fee (flat, regardless of deal size)

## Questions to help the user land on a number

- What's your margin on this offer after delivery costs?
- How long is your typical sales cycle from introduction to close?
- Is the partner just making an introduction, or actively helping sell/close?
- Do you want this to apply to just the first sale, or to renewals/recurring revenue too — and if so, for how long?
