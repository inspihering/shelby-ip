# The CORE Messaging Framework — full detail

## C — Clarity

Clarify the message before amplifying it.

Focus on:
- What they do
- Who they serve
- The problem they solve
- The transformation they create
- The promise of their offer
- The outcome their audience wants
- The language their audience already uses

**Key idea:** If people can't quickly understand the value, they can't confidently buy, refer, or remember it.

Guiding questions:
- What's the simplest way to describe this offer?
- What result does the client actually want?
- What pain point is being solved?
- What's the most urgent message the market needs to hear right now?
- What's the one-sentence positioning statement?

## O — Ownership

Help the user own their expertise, perspective, story, and differentiator.

Focus on:
- Credibility
- Authority
- Lived experience
- Professional expertise
- Distinct point of view
- Signature frameworks
- Personal brand story
- Thought leadership position

**Key idea:** The competitive edge isn't just what someone knows — it's how they own their perspective and communicate why it matters.

Guiding questions:
- What makes this person credible?
- What story explains why this work matters to them?
- What unique perspective do they bring?
- What do they believe that challenges common thinking?
- What's their signature idea, phrase, or framework?

## R — Relevance

Connect the message to the needs, desires, problems, and priorities of the ideal audience.

Focus on:
- Audience psychology
- Buyer awareness
- Industry needs
- Pain points
- Desired transformation
- Emotional connection
- Market timing
- Decision-maker priorities

**Key idea:** The message becomes powerful when the audience can see themselves in it.

Guiding questions:
- What does the audience care about right now?
- What problem are they actively trying to solve?
- What language would make them feel understood?
- What's at stake if they don't solve the problem?
- How does this connect to a current marketplace need?

## E — Engagement

Turn the message into content, conversations, offers, and visibility strategies that invite action.

Focus on:
- Calls to action
- Content strategy
- Speaking topics
- Social media posts
- Email campaigns
- Sales conversations
- Community engagement
- Lead magnets
- Keynote messaging
- Offer invitations

**Key idea:** A strong message doesn't just explain the business — it opens a relationship with the right audience.

Guiding questions:
- What action should the audience take next?
- How does this become a post, video, keynote, email, or offer?
- How does this message invite conversation?
- How does this content build trust over time?
- How does the message lead naturally into the offer?
